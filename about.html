About me

My journey started in July 2024, when I started exploring AI tools on YouTube. One day, I came across a video that was talking about building your own AI models. What intrigued me was that I could start learning Python first, which is a comparatively easier language to start the journey into the AI field. Watching practical tutorials and step-by-step guides convinced me that learning Python would be the fastest way to build useful tools. I committed to daily practice and focused learning: small, consistent steps that compound over time.

I decided to dedicate at least 30 minutes per day for a month to see if I am committed to learning Python. I started with the book called Python Crash Course by Eric Matthes. Eventually, those 30 minutes turned into one hour, two hours, three hours... and consequently, my curiosity led me deeper into the path towards data science. Additionally, I analyzed that to learn machine learning models, I needed to learn linear algebra, matrices, vectors, and statistics. So I started taking lessons from Khan Academy, which was the best free option.

Once I finished the prerequisites of machine learning, I then completed several Kaggle micro-courses (Intro to ML, Intermediate ML, Feature Engineering) and began taking part in Kaggle Playground competitions. I enjoy taking part in Kaggle competitions since you get exposure to world-class solutions from competitors around the world and share their insights about the dataset in discussion. One of the best approaches to the competition was to take part in the discussion and implement the recommended techniques since you get to learn a lot from them.

I followed a practice-first approach inspired by Atomic Habits: daily progress, measurable practice, and consistent review. Today, I am focused on machine learning, AI, and clear visual storytelling. I believe data is more than numbers — every dataset has a story. My goal is to continue learning and keep improving myself to create impactful solutions that will make our society better.
