About me

Since my childhood, I have been on an expedition to overcome my inhibitions and master complex disciplines, a challenging yet fascinating pursuit. My name is Shubham Jain, and I was born in New Delhi, India. Now, I am living in Calgary, Canada, where I am building a unique career path at the intersection of accounting, finance, and technology.

During my schooling years in India, my classmates often made fun of my speaking skills. Back then, I frequently stammered and made grammatical mistakes, which became a source of ridicule. This experience always inhibited me from taking the first step toward improvement, leaving me with a deep-seated fear of speaking English. In 2016, I moved to Charlottetown, Canada, and faced a new challenge: I could not speak English confidently because of the lingering inhibition from my school years. It hindered my ability to make friends or even order food at a restaurant. However, I refused to let fear define my limits. I realized that overcoming a daunting challenge simply requires breaking it down into smaller, manageable steps.

This same philosophy of breaking down complex problems guided my professional life. I built a strong academic foundation, earning my Bachelor of Business Administration in Accounting and pursuing the CPA designation. Over four years in Calgary's corporate sector, I managed large financial datasets, performed rigorous reconciliations in NetSuite, and ensured absolute data integrity for month-end closes. I learned to love working with data, identifying hidden discrepancies, and standardizing chaotic workflows. Through this process, I discovered a core truth: Behind every number in a ledger is a larger business story waiting to be told. But while accounting taught me how to record the past perfectly, I found myself increasingly curious about predicting the future.

In July 2024, a new chapter began. While exploring AI tools on YouTube, I came across a video about building your own AI models. What intrigued me was that I could start learning Python first, which is a comparatively easier language to begin the journey into the AI field. Watching practical tutorials and step-by-step guides convinced me that learning Python would be the fastest way to build useful tools. I decided to dedicate at least 30 minutes per day for a month to see if I was truly committed to this new path. I started with the book called Python Crash Course by Eric Matthes. I followed a practice-first approach inspired by Atomic Habits: daily progress, measurable practice, and consistent review. Eventually, those 30 minutes turned into one hour, then two, then three, and, consequently, my curiosity led me deeper down the path toward data science. I realized that to truly learn machine learning, I needed to understand linear algebra, matrices, vectors, and statistics. So, I went back to basics and started taking lessons from Khan Academy, which was the best free option available.

Once I finished the prerequisites of machine learning, I completed several Kaggle micro-courses covering Intro to ML, Intermediate ML, and Feature Engineering. I then began taking part in Kaggle Playground competitions. I enjoy participating in Kaggle competitions because I get exposure to world-class solutions from competitors worldwide. One of the best approaches to the competition was to actively participate in the discussion forums, read others' insights on the dataset, and implement their recommended techniques. You get to learn so much from the global community when you approach data with humility.

Today, I stand at a unique crossroads. I am a hybrid professional who bridges the gap between the boardroom and the server room. I can speak the language of financial statements and variance analysis just as fluently as I can speak the language of Python libraries and SQL queries. I am focused on machine learning, AI, and clear visual storytelling. I believe data is more than just numbers on a screen. Every dataset has a story. My goal is to continue learning, keep improving, and use my hybrid skill set to create impactful solutions that will improve our society.
